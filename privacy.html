Privacy Policy

Effective 10 June 2026

Trimate is a triathlon training app. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the control you have over it. The short version: your data is used only to provide your training experience, it is never sold, and it is never used for advertising.

Data we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (handled by our authentication provider; we never see or store your password in plain text).
  • Profile & training data: the details you enter to build your plan — body metrics (e.g. weight, height, age, biological sex), fitness baselines, race goals, your training plan, and the workouts you complete (duration, distance, heart rate, pace, power).
  • Nutrition data: dietary preferences, exclusions, and meal/fueling logs, if you use the Fuel features.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Trimate reads data such as workouts, heart rate, and activity from Apple Health to personalize your plan and record guided sessions, and saves completed sessions back to Health. HealthKit data is never used for advertising or marketing, is never sold, and is never shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings.

Connected services (Strava, Garmin, COROS)

If you choose to connect a service like Strava, Garmin, or COROS, Trimate uses that connection — authorized by you via OAuth — to send your planned workouts to your watch and/or receive your completed activities, so sessions are marked done automatically and your training load stays accurate. You can disconnect at any time in the app, which deletes the connection and its tokens. If you revoke access from the provider's side, we honour it and remove the provider's data.

Where your data lives

Data is stored in our database hosted by Supabase (PostgreSQL), encrypted in transit and at rest, with row-level security so each record is accessible only to the account that owns it. We use Sentry for crash reporting (technical error data, not your training content) and Expo (EAS) to deliver app updates. These processors act on our instructions; none of them may use your data for their own purposes.

What we don't do

  • No selling of personal data — to anyone, ever.
  • No advertising or ad tracking in the app.
  • No sharing with third parties beyond the processors and connections described above.

Retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account), which permanently removes your account and all associated data — profile, plans, workouts, activities, nutrition logs, and service connections.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK/EU GDPR), you have rights to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data. Email us and we'll help.
